XIMPAX is a boutique supply chain and procurement consultancy headquartered in Switzerland.
We serve FMCG, Life Sciences, and specialty manufacturing companies across Europe.

WHAT WE DO:
- Embedded Expert Deployment: We place senior supply chain and procurement professionals
  directly inside client organisations on a project or interim basis — day-one ready,
  no ramp-up overhead.
- Category Management & Source-to-Pay: End-to-end category strategy, supplier negotiations,
  RFP management, and Source-to-Pay process design, delivering measurable cost savings.
- Operational Excellence: S&OP/IBP design, demand planning, network design, process
  optimisation, and make-vs-buy analysis.
- Transformation Support: Post-merger supply chain integration, nearshoring strategy,
  ERP-adjacent procurement process redesign, and capability building.

WHO WE WORK WITH:
Chief Procurement Officers, VP Supply Chain, Directors of Operations, and Heads of Category
Management at mid-to-large enterprises undergoing transformation, growth, or disruption.

OUR DIFFERENTIATOR:
We are practitioners, not theorists. Every XIMPAX expert has held senior line roles in
the industries we serve. We embed, execute, and transfer knowledge — we do not deliver
slide decks.

TONE IN OUTREACH:
Confident but not boastful. Peer-to-peer. We understand the operational reality they face.
We do not pitch — we open a conversation about their specific challenge.
